### FAQs

**Q: Can I use this if I have sensitive skin?**
A: Vitamin C can cause tingling, especially for sensitive skin. Start by using every other day and build up to daily use. If irritation persists, discontinue use.

**Q: Why does the color change over time?**
A: Pure L-ascorbic acid oxidizes when exposed to light and air, turning slightly yellow. This is normal. As long as it's not dark brown, it's still effective. Store properly to minimize oxidation.

**Q: Can I use this with retinol?**
A: Yes, but we recommend vitamin C in the morning and retinol at night. Using both in one routine can be irritating for some skin types.

**Q: How long will one bottle last?**
A: With daily use (3-4 drops), one 30ml bottle lasts approximately 2-3 months.

**Q: Why is it more expensive than some vitamin C serums?**
A: We use the most effective form (L-ascorbic acid at 15%), organic supporting ingredients, and sustainable packaging. We never compromise on quality to hit a price point.

### Products & Ingredients

**Q: Are your products truly organic?**
A: All our primary ingredients are certified organic (Ecocert). Some supporting ingredients (like vitamin C) can't be organic because they're synthesized. We're transparent about which ingredients are organic and which aren't.

**Q: Why do you use some synthetic ingredients?**
A: Some ingredients—like pure L-ascorbic acid (vitamin C) and hyaluronic acid—can't be sourced from plants in a stable, effective form. We use biofermented or synthesized versions when they're more effective or sustainable than natural alternatives.

**Q: Can I use your products during pregnancy?**
A: Most of our products are pregnancy-safe, but we recommend consulting your healthcare provider, especially regarding vitamin C serums and active ingredients.

---

### Transparency

**Q: Why don't you disclose exact formulas?**
A: We disclose all ingredients and their approximate percentages (rounded to nearest 0.5%). We don't disclose the exact formula down to 0.01% precision, as this would allow direct copying. We balance transparency with protecting our formulation work.

**Q: How do you verify your suppliers?**
A: We require organic certifications, visit farms when possible, and conduct third-party testing on all ingredient batches.

---

### Sustainability

**Q: How are you carbon neutral?**
A: We calculate emissions from ingredient sourcing, production, packaging, and shipping. We offset 100% through verified Nordic forest conservation projects.

**Q: Are your bottles recyclable?**
A: Yes. Glass bottles are infinitely recyclable. Please recycle them. We're also developing a refill program.

---

### Returns & Satisfaction

**Q: What if I don't like a product?**
A: We offer a 90-day money-back guarantee. If you're not satisfied, return it (even if partially used) for a full refund.

**Q: Why 90 days?**
A: Skincare takes time to work. We want you to give our products a fair trial—at least 8 weeks—before deciding.
