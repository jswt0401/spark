# PureGlow Launch Campaign - Project Plan

**Project Manager**: Spark AI Team - Project Management Agent
**Client**: PureGlow
**Project**: Brand Launch Campaign
**Duration**: 12 weeks (January 16 - April 1, 2026)
**Budget**: €75,000

---

## Executive Summary

This project plan outlines the comprehensive 12-week launch campaign for PureGlow, a new organic skincare brand. The campaign will establish brand identity, build market awareness, and drive initial customer acquisition through integrated digital marketing efforts.

## Project Objectives

### Primary Objectives
1. ✅ Launch PureGlow brand with strong market presence
2. ✅ Achieve 50,000 website visitors in first 3 months
3. ✅ Generate €100,000 in revenue within first 3 months post-launch
4. ✅ Build social media following of 25K+ across platforms
5. ✅ Establish brand as trusted, science-based organic skincare leader

### Success Criteria
- Campaign launches on-time (April 1, 2026)
- All deliverables meet quality standards
- Budget maintained within 5% variance
- Client satisfaction score: 9/10+
- Post-launch conversion rate: 2.5%+

## Risk Management

### High-Priority Risks

**Risk 1: Timeline Delays**
- **Probability**: Medium
- **Impact**: High
- **Mitigation**: Buffer time built into schedule, early identification of blockers
- **Contingency**: Phased launch if necessary

**Risk 2: Scope Creep**
- **Probability**: High
- **Impact**: Medium
- **Mitigation**: Clear scope documentation, change request process
- **Contingency**: Prioritization framework for additions

**Risk 3: Content Approval Delays**
- **Probability**: Medium
- **Impact**: Medium
- **Mitigation**: Clear approval timeline, limited revision rounds
- **Contingency**: Parallel work streams where possible

**Risk 4: Budget Overrun**
- **Probability**: Low
- **Impact**: High
- **Mitigation**: Strict budget tracking, regular reviews
- **Contingency**: Contingency fund, scope adjustment

**Risk 5: Low Initial Performance**
- **Probability**: Medium
- **Impact**: Medium
- **Mitigation**: Conservative projections, optimization budget
- **Contingency**: Rapid testing and iteration plan
