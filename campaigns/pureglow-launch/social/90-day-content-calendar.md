## Community Management Guidelines

### Response Time Goals

- **Comments**: Within 4 hours during business hours
- **DMs**: Within 24 hours
- **Reviews**: Within 48 hours

### Response Tone

- Warm and helpful
- Educational when possible
- Never defensive
- Acknowledge criticism honestly
- Provide solutions

### Handling Negative Comments

1. **Acknowledge**: "Thank you for your feedback"
2. **Understand**: Ask clarifying questions
3. **Solve**: Offer solution (refund, replacement, advice)
4. **Move to DM**: "Let's continue this in DM"
5. **Follow up**: Ensure resolution

### What to Delete

- Spam
- Hate speech
- Dangerous misinformation
- Explicit content

### What NOT to Delete

- Negative reviews (respond constructively)
- Questions or concerns
- Constructive criticism
- Comparison to competitors
