# PureGlow Launch Campaign - Creative Concept

**Campaign Name**: "Glow from Within, Truth from the Start"
**Creative Director**: Spark AI Team
**Campaign Duration**: 12 weeks (January - April 2026)
**Launch Date**: April 1, 2026
**Status**: APPROVED ✅

---

## Executive Summary

The "Glow from Within, Truth from the Start" campaign introduces PureGlow as the first truly transparent organic skincare brand. The creative concept centers on revealing what's hidden in the beauty industry—from ingredient percentages to sourcing locations—while showing that transparency builds both trust and results.

**Campaign Pillars**:
1. **Radical Transparency** - Show everything others hide
2. **Science Meets Nature** - Prove organic works
3. **Nordic Authenticity** - From our forests to your skin

---

## 1. Campaign Concept

### 1.1 Big Idea

**"What if skincare showed its work?"**

Just like a math teacher asks students to show their work, PureGlow shows the work behind every product: the research, the percentages, the sources, the science. We don't just claim transparency—we prove it.

### 1.2 Creative Platform

**Visual Concept**: Layered transparency

Physical and metaphorical layers being revealed:
- Transparent product bottles showing contents
- Infographics revealing ingredient breakdowns
- Before/after showing transformation
- Behind-the-scenes revealing sourcing
- See-through overlays revealing information

**Narrative Concept**: The reveal

Every piece of content is structured as a reveal—showing customers what brands usually hide:
- "Here's what's really in your serum"
- "This is where our ingredients come from"
- "These are the studies that prove it works"
- "This is exactly what it costs to make"

### 1.3 Campaign Mood

**Emotion**: Empowerment through knowledge
**Tone**: Confident, warm, educational
**Aesthetic**: Clean, modern, Nordic minimalism
**Feeling**: "Finally, a brand that respects my intelligence"

### 8.2 Ad Creative Guidelines

**Do**:
✅ Use real customer photos (with permission)
✅ Show actual products
✅ Include specific facts (percentages, sources)
✅ Use clean, minimal design
✅ Make text readable on mobile
✅ Front-load key message (first 3 seconds)

**Don't**:
❌ Use stock photography
❌ Overpromise results
❌ Use before/after photos with different lighting
❌ Cluttered design
❌ Generic beauty claims
❌ Focus on price/discounts primarily
