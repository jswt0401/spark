## 6. Challenges & Learnings

### 6.1 Challenges Faced

**Challenge 1: Paid Social CPM Increase**
- **Issue**: CPMs increased 22% in May due to seasonal competition
- **Impact**: Required budget reallocation to maintain efficiency
- **Solution**: Shifted more budget to TikTok (lower CPMs), increased organic efforts

**Challenge 2: Product Availability**
- **Issue**: Glow Essence Serum sold out for 4 days in late May
- **Impact**: Lost sales, some customer frustration
- **Solution**: Improved inventory forecasting, set up back-in-stock notifications

**Challenge 3: Customer Education on Active Ingredients**
- **Issue**: Some customers confused/concerned about vitamin C tingling
- **Impact**: 12% of reviews mentioned it
- **Solution**: Enhanced product page with "what to expect," better email onboarding

**Challenge 4: Cold Start for Retargeting**
- **Issue**: First 2 weeks had limited retargeting pools
- **Impact**: Lower ROAS in early weeks
- **Solution**: Expected issue, improved as traffic grew

### 6.2 Key Learnings

**1. Transparency Resonates Strongly**
- Content featuring ingredient disclosure, sourcing, studies consistently outperformed
- Validates core brand positioning
- Continue doubling down on this differentiator

**2. Education Drives Higher-Quality Customers**
- Educational content had highest conversion rate (3.5%) and lowest return rate
- Informed customers are better customers
- Invest more in educational content

**3. Micro-Influencers > Macro for D2C**
- Better engagement, more authentic, lower cost
- Shift future influencer budget: 80% micro, 20% macro

**4. Email is Highly Efficient**
- Best channel ROI and conversion rate
- Prioritize list growth
- Increase email frequency (currently weekly, could do 2x/week)

**5. Organic Social Takes Time But Delivers**
- First month slow, but accelerated in months 2-3
- Viral moments happen organically with consistent quality
- Continue investing in consistent, value-driven content

**6. Bundles Drive Higher LTV**
- Bundle customers worth 2.8x more
- Introduce more bundle options
- Educate on routine building to encourage multi-product purchase
