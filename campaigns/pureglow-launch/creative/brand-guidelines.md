## 1. Brand Overview

### 1.1 Brand Essence

**PureGlow** is the skincare brand that empowers consumers through radical transparency and science-backed organic beauty.

**Brand Mantra**: "Your skin deserves transparency"

### 1.2 Brand Purpose

To eliminate confusion and greenwashing in the beauty industry by providing complete transparency about ingredients, sourcing, and science—proving that organic can be both ethical and effective.

### 1.3 Brand Values

**TRANSPARENT**
Everything disclosed. Nothing hidden. Full ingredient percentages, sourcing locations, and scientific backing published openly.

**EFFECTIVE**
Science-backed formulations with proven results. Clinical testing and real customer outcomes drive every product decision.

**ETHICAL**
Truly organic, sustainably sourced, cruelty-free, and vegan. Environmental responsibility at every step.

**EDUCATIONAL**
Empowering customers with knowledge about ingredients, skincare science, and product choices.

### 1.4 Brand Personality

**Adjectives that describe PureGlow**:
- Honest & transparent
- Scientific yet accessible
- Sophisticated but approachable
- Calm & trustworthy
- Minimalist & intentional
- Progressive & innovative
- Empowering & educational

**If PureGlow were a person**:
A thoughtful, well-educated scientist who's passionate about making complex information accessible. They're the friend who does the research for you, explains things clearly without condescension, and genuinely wants you to make informed choices. They're quietly confident, never pushy, and value substance over style.

### 2.1 Logo

**Primary Logo**:
```
┌─────────────────────┐
│                     │
│   PG               │
│   ──────           │
│   PUREGLOW         │
│                     │
```

**Description**:
- Monogram "PG" in clean, modern sans-serif
- Minimalist underline element
- Full wordmark "PUREGLOW" in all caps, letter-spaced
- Sage green (#B8C5B0) on off-white background

**Logo Variations**:

1. **Primary** - Full color with monogram and wordmark
2. **Monogram only** - "PG" mark (for small applications, social avatars)
3. **Wordmark only** - "PUREGLOW" text (for headers, packaging labels)
4. **Reversed** - White logo on dark backgrounds
5. **Monochrome** - Charcoal or black for specific applications

### 2.2 Logo Usage

**Clear Space**:
- Minimum clear space: Height of the "G" on all sides
- Never crowd the logo with text or images

**Minimum Sizes**:
- Digital: 120px width
- Print: 25mm width
- Monogram standalone: 40px digital, 10mm print

**Logo Don'ts**:
❌ Do not stretch or distort
❌ Do not change colors (except approved variations)
❌ Do not add effects (shadows, gradients, outlines)
❌ Do not place on busy backgrounds
❌ Do not rotate or angle
❌ Do not alter spacing or proportions

## 3. Visual Identity

### 3.1 Design Principles

**Minimalism with Purpose**
Every element serves a function. Clean layouts with generous white space. Let the content and products breathe.

**Transparency as Design**
Visual representations of transparency: layered elements, see-through graphics, ingredient breakdowns, percentages visible.

**Science Meets Nature**
Balance organic, natural imagery with clean, scientific information design. Charts, data, and formulas presented beautifully.

**Nordic Simplicity**
Inspired by Scandinavian design: functional, beautiful, unpretentious. Nature-inspired without being overly "earthy."

### 3.2 Visual Style

**Aesthetic Keywords**:
- Clean & minimal
- Organic & natural
- Scientific & precise
- Calm & soothing
- Modern & fresh
- Trustworthy & professional

**Design Elements**:

**Shapes**: Soft circles, gentle curves, organic forms. Avoid harsh angles or aggressive shapes.

**Textures**: Subtle, natural textures (paper, linen, water) used sparingly. Never overwhelming.

**Patterns**: Botanical illustrations, molecular structures, ingredient cross-sections. Simple, line-based.

**Graphics**: Clean infographics, ingredient percentage breakdowns, scientific diagrams, transparent overlays.

**Photography**: See Section 6

## 4. Typography

### 4.1 Primary Typeface: Inter

**Usage**: All digital applications, website, social media, emails

**Inter** - Modern, clean sans-serif with excellent readability
- **Headings**: Inter Bold (700), all caps for major headings
- **Subheadings**: Inter SemiBold (600), sentence case
- **Body Text**: Inter Regular (400), comfortable line spacing (1.6)
- **Captions**: Inter Regular (400), smaller size
- **Emphasis**: Inter Medium (500)

**Type Scale** (Website):
```
H1: 48px / 56px line height / Inter Bold
H2: 36px / 44px line height / Inter SemiBold
H3: 28px / 36px line height / Inter SemiBold
H4: 22px / 30px line height / Inter Medium
Body: 16px / 26px line height / Inter Regular
Small: 14px / 22px line height / Inter Regular
Caption: 12px / 18px line height / Inter Regular
```

### 4.2 Secondary Typeface: Crimson Pro (Print only)

**Usage**: Premium print applications, packaging, editorial content

**Crimson Pro** - Elegant serif for premium touch
- **Headings**: Crimson Pro SemiBold
- **Body**: Crimson Pro Regular

### 4.3 Typography Guidelines

**Do**:
✅ Use generous line spacing (1.6 for body text)
✅ Use sentence case for most content (all caps for headings only)
✅ Maintain hierarchy through size and weight, not color alone
✅ Keep line length 60-80 characters for readability
✅ Use bold sparingly for emphasis

**Don't**:
❌ Use more than 2 font families together
❌ Use italic Inter (use Medium weight instead)
❌ Set body text smaller than 14px
❌ Use decorative or script fonts
❌ Underline text (except links)

## 5. Color Palette

### 5.1 Primary Colors

**Sage Green** - Primary brand color
- HEX: #B8C5B0
- RGB: 184, 197, 176
- CMYK: 20, 5, 25, 0
- Usage: Logo, primary CTAs, accents, headers

**Off-White** - Primary background
- HEX: #FAF9F6
- RGB: 250, 249, 246
- CMYK: 2, 2, 4, 0
- Usage: Backgrounds, negative space, paper

**Charcoal** - Primary text
- HEX: #36454F
- RGB: 54, 69, 79
- CMYK: 70, 55, 45, 45
- Usage: Body text, headlines, icons

### 5.2 Secondary Colors

**Forest Green** - Accent
- HEX: #6B8E65
- RGB: 107, 142, 101
- CMYK: 45, 15, 50, 5
- Usage: Secondary CTAs, highlights, active states

**Soft Grey** - Supporting
- HEX: #E8E8E8
- RGB: 232, 232, 232
- CMYK: 8, 6, 7, 0
- Usage: Borders, dividers, subtle backgrounds

**Warm Beige** - Accent
- HEX: #EDE7D9
- RGB: 237, 231, 217
- CMYK: 6, 6, 13, 0
- Usage: Callouts, highlight boxes, warm accents

### 5.3 Semantic Colors

**Success/Positive**
- Green: #6B8E65 (Forest Green)
- Usage: Checkmarks, success messages, positive indicators

**Warning/Attention**
- Amber: #D4A574
- Usage: Warnings, important notices

**Error/Alert**
- Terracotta: #C17D6A
- Usage: Error messages, urgent notices (use sparingly)

**Information**
- Sage: #B8C5B0
- Usage: Info boxes, tips, educational content

### 5.4 Color Usage Guidelines

**Do**:
✅ Use Sage Green for brand identity and primary actions
✅ Use Off-White as primary background for clean, airy feel
✅ Use Charcoal for all body text (excellent readability)
✅ Use color intentionally to guide user attention
✅ Maintain sufficient contrast for accessibility (WCAG AA minimum)

**Don't**:
❌ Use bright, saturated colors (off-brand)
❌ Use black #000000 (use Charcoal instead)
❌ Use pure white #FFFFFF (use Off-White for warmth)
❌ Use more than 3 colors in a single design
❌ Use color as the only way to convey information

## 6. Photography & Imagery

### 6.1 Photography Style

**Aesthetic**:
- Natural, soft lighting (golden hour, diffused)
- Minimal styling and props
- Focus on products and ingredients in natural settings
- Authentic, unretouched skin (real texture visible)
- Nordic landscapes and botanicals
- Clean, organized product shots

**Mood**:
- Calm and serene
- Fresh and clean
- Aspirational but achievable
- Authentic and honest

### 6.2 Subject Matter

**Products**:
- Clean backgrounds (white, beige, natural surfaces)
- Natural materials (wood, marble, linen)
- Botanical elements (fresh ingredients)
- Transparent containers visible
- Minimal styling (no clutter)

**People**:
- Diverse ages, skin tones, and genders
- Natural expressions (subtle smiles, contemplative)
- Real skin texture (no excessive retouching)
- Minimal makeup
- Natural settings (bedrooms, bathrooms, outdoors)
- Close-ups of skin application

**Ingredients**:
- Fresh, organic ingredients
- Nordic botanicals (sea buckthorn, chamomile, birch)
- Macro photography of plants
- Cross-sections showing ingredients
- Ingredients in natural habitats

**Lifestyle**:
- Minimalist Nordic interiors
- Natural outdoor settings
- Morning/evening routines
- Sustainable living moments
- Hands applying products (diverse skin tones)

### 6.3 Photography Don'ts

❌ Overly styled or artificial-looking shots
❌ Heavy filters or excessive editing
❌ Cluttered backgrounds
❌ Aggressive poses or expressions
❌ Stock photo "fakeness"
❌ Overly retouched skin (show real texture)
❌ Dark, moody lighting
❌ Busy patterns or distracting elements

### 6.4 Image Treatments

**Filters**: None or minimal. Slightly lifted shadows, natural color correction only.

**Overlays**: Semi-transparent Sage Green overlay (20% opacity) for text-heavy images.

**Borders**: No borders on images. Let them breathe.

**Aspect Ratios**:
- Instagram Feed: 1:1 (square)
- Instagram Stories: 9:16 (vertical)
- Website Hero: 16:9 (landscape)
- Product Images: 1:1 or 4:5

## 7. Brand Voice & Tone

### 7.1 Brand Voice

The PureGlow voice is:

**TRANSPARENT**
- We explain everything clearly
- We use simple language for complex topics
- We cite sources and show our work
- We admit limitations honestly

**EDUCATED & EDUCATIONAL**
- We're knowledgeable but not condescending
- We teach without lecturing
- We use scientific terms but always explain them
- We empower customers with information

**CONFIDENT BUT HUMBLE**
- We're proud of our transparency and quality
- We don't claim to be perfect
- We acknowledge other good options exist
- We let our actions speak louder than claims

**WARM & APPROACHABLE**
- We're friendly without being overly casual
- We're professional without being stiff
- We're helpful, never pushy
- We're conversational but not trendy

### 7.2 Tone Variations by Context

**Website Homepage** - Confident & Welcoming
"Welcome to skincare that shows its work. Every ingredient, every percentage, every source—fully disclosed."

**Product Pages** - Informative & Detailed
"Glow Essence Serum contains 15% stable vitamin C (L-ascorbic acid) sourced from Scotland. Here's why that matters..."

**Blog Articles** - Educational & Accessible
"Let's talk about hyaluronic acid. Despite the scary-sounding name, it's actually a molecule your skin already makes..."

**Social Media** - Conversational & Engaging
"Fun fact: Our sea buckthorn comes from organic farms in Finnish Lapland. Here's why location matters for ingredient quality 🌿"

**Email** - Personal & Helpful
"Hi [Name], We noticed you're interested in vitamin C serums. Here's what you should know before choosing one..."

**Customer Service** - Patient & Solution-Oriented
"I'm happy to help you find the right product for your skin. Can you tell me more about your concerns?"

### 7.3 Writing Guidelines

**Do**:
✅ Use "we" and "you" to create conversation
✅ Start sentences with verbs when giving instructions
✅ Use contractions naturally (we're, you're, it's)
✅ Ask questions to engage readers
✅ Break complex topics into digestible pieces
✅ Cite sources and link to studies
✅ Use active voice
✅ Write in second person (you) for instructions

**Don't**:
❌ Use jargon without explanation
❌ Make unsubstantiated claims
❌ Use trendy slang or excessive emoji
❌ Write in brand-speak or marketing clichés
❌ Use fear-based messaging
❌ Exaggerate or oversell
❌ Compare negatively to competitors by name
❌ Use passive voice unnecessarily

### 7.4 Vocabulary

**Words We Use**:
- Transparent, clear, disclosed
- Science-backed, clinically-tested, proven
- Effective, working, functional
- Organic, sustainable, ethical
- Honest, authentic, real
- Empower, inform, educate

**Words We Avoid**:
- Revolutionary, groundbreaking (overused)
- Miracle, magical (unrealistic)
- Toxic, dangerous (fear-based)
- Anti-aging (prefer "age-supportive")
- Chemical-free (inaccurate—water is a chemical)
- All-natural (too vague)
- Detox, cleanse (scientifically questionable)

## 8. Messaging Framework

### 8.1 Core Brand Message

**Primary Message**:
"Your skin deserves transparency. We prove everything we claim."

### 8.2 Key Messages by Audience

**For the Ingredient-Conscious**:
"See exactly what's in your skincare—down to the percentage and source. No hidden ingredients, no vague terms, just full disclosure."

**For the Science-Minded**:
"Every ingredient backed by published research. Every claim linked to the study. Organic doesn't mean unproven—it means both effective and ethical."

**For the Sustainability-Focused**:
"Carbon-neutral from farm to doorstep. Organic ingredients sourced responsibly. Recyclable glass packaging. Certified and verified at every step."

**For the Skeptical**:
"We understand your doubts—the beauty industry has earned them. That's why we publish everything. Check our claims. Read the studies. We welcome scrutiny."

### 8.3 Product Messaging Formula

**Structure**: [What it is] + [What it does] + [Why it works] + [Proof]

**Example**:
"Glow Essence Serum is a daily vitamin C serum (WHAT) that brightens skin and reduces visible aging signs (DOES). Formulated with 15% stable L-ascorbic acid and vitamin E, ingredients clinically shown to boost collagen production (WHY). See the published studies → (PROOF)"

### 8.4 Campaign Taglines

**Launch Campaign**: "Glow from Within, Truth from the Start"
**Transparency Campaign**: "Nothing to Hide, Everything to Share"
**Science Campaign**: "Organic. Proven. Both."
**Sustainability Campaign**: "From Our Forests to Your Skin"

## 10. Brand Don'ts

### 10.1 Visual Don'ts

❌ **Don't** use bright, neon, or overly saturated colors
❌ **Don't** use busy patterns or overwhelming designs
❌ **Don't** use decorative fonts or excessive text effects
❌ **Don't** crowd designs (respect white space)
❌ **Don't** use clichéd beauty imagery (water splashes, perfect skin with no texture)
❌ **Don't** use stock photos that look generic
❌ **Don't** use filters that distort natural colors

### 10.2 Voice & Messaging Don'ts

❌ **Don't** make claims you can't back up with studies
❌ **Don't** use fear-based marketing ("toxic chemicals")
❌ **Don't** talk down to customers
❌ **Don't** use trendy internet slang excessively
❌ **Don't** name competitors negatively
❌ **Don't** promise overnight miracles
❌ **Don't** use vague terms like "all-natural"
❌ **Don't** hide behind marketing speak

### 10.3 Brand Behavior Don'ts

❌ **Don't** delete negative reviews (address them transparently)
❌ **Don't** make sustainability claims without certifications
❌ **Don't** hide ingredient sources or percentages
❌ **Don't** use scientific language to confuse
❌ **Don't** follow every trend (stay true to brand values)
❌ **Don't** oversell or pressure customers
❌ **Don't** ignore customer questions or concerns

## 11. Brand Checklist

Before publishing any branded material, check:

**Visual**:
□ Uses approved colors from palette
□ Uses Inter typeface (or approved alternatives)
□ Logo properly sized and placed with clear space
□ Photography style aligns with guidelines
□ Design is clean and uncluttered
□ Accessible contrast ratios (WCAG AA minimum)

**Messaging**:
□ Tone is warm, transparent, and educational
□ Claims are backed by sources
□ No marketing clichés or jargon
□ Active voice and clear language
□ Inclusive and accessible
□ Aligns with brand values

**Brand Integrity**:
□ Represents products honestly
□ Supports transparency mission
□ Educates rather than pressures
□ Shows respect for customer intelligence
□ Maintains consistent voice
□ Cites sources where appropriate
