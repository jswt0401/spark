# Agents

This directory contains AI agent configurations, personalities, and role definitions for the Spark team.

## Purpose

Define specialized AI agents that work together to deliver marketing solutions.

## Team Overview

The Spark AI work team consists of seven specialized agents, each with unique expertise and responsibilities. Together, they form a complete marketing agency capable of delivering comprehensive campaigns and strategies.

## Available Agents

### 🎯 [Strategist](./strategist.md)
**Role:** Strategic Planning & Analysis
**Focus:** Marketing strategy, market research, competitive intelligence, campaign planning

The Strategist is the strategic mind behind campaigns, developing data-driven marketing strategies that align with business objectives.

### ✍️ [Copywriter](./copywriter.md)
**Role:** Content Creation & Writing
**Focus:** Compelling copy, brand messaging, content across all channels

The Copywriter crafts persuasive, engaging copy that connects with audiences and drives action across all marketing channels.

### 🎨 [Creative Director](./creative-director.md)
**Role:** Creative Leadership & Direction
**Focus:** Creative concepts, brand stewardship, visual strategy, quality control

The Creative Director is the visionary leader of creative execution, ensuring innovative concepts and brand consistency.

### 📊 [Analyst](./analyst.md)
**Role:** Data Analysis & Insights
**Focus:** Performance tracking, data reporting, optimization recommendations, ROI measurement

The Analyst transforms raw data into actionable insights that drive marketing decisions and optimize campaign performance.

### 📱 [Social Media Manager](./social-media-manager.md)
**Role:** Social Media & Community Management
**Focus:** Social strategy, content creation, community engagement, platform expertise

The Social Media Manager builds communities and drives engagement across social platforms.

### 🔍 [SEO Specialist](./seo-specialist.md)
**Role:** Search Engine Optimization
**Focus:** Organic visibility, keyword research, technical SEO, content optimization

The SEO Specialist improves search rankings and drives organic traffic through strategic optimization.

### 📋 [Project Manager](./project-manager.md)
**Role:** Project Management & Coordination
**Focus:** Workflow coordination, timeline management, quality assurance, stakeholder communication

The Project Manager orchestrates the team, ensuring deliverables are met on time and to specification.

## Collaboration

All agents work together following established protocols outlined in the [Collaboration Guide](./collaboration-guide.md).

### Key Collaboration Principles:
- Clear communication and documentation
- Defined handoffs and quality criteria
- Collaborative decision-making
- Mutual support and knowledge sharing
- Continuous improvement

## Getting Started

### Working with Agents

1. **Understand Roles**: Review individual agent profiles to understand capabilities
2. **Follow Workflows**: Use the collaboration guide for multi-agent projects
3. **Clear Briefs**: Provide comprehensive briefs with objectives and requirements
4. **Feedback Loops**: Establish clear review and approval processes
5. **Track Progress**: Use the Project Manager for coordination

### For New Projects

1. **Kickoff**: Project Manager initiates and defines scope
2. **Strategy**: Strategist develops approach with input from relevant agents
3. **Creation**: Creative agents execute deliverables
4. **Optimization**: Analyst tracks performance, agents optimize
5. **Retrospective**: Team learns and improves
