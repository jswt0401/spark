# PureGlow - Client Profile

## Company Overview

**Company Name**: PureGlow
**Industry**: Beauty & Personal Care - Organic Skincare
**Founded**: 2025
**Headquarters**: Copenhagen, Denmark
**Company Stage**: Pre-launch startup

## Business Model

- **Type**: Direct-to-Consumer (D2C) + Selective Retail
- **Distribution**: E-commerce primary, 10 boutique partners planned
- **Price Point**: Premium (€35-85 per product)
- **Product Range**: 5 core products at launch

## Brand Identity

### Mission
"Making science-based, truly organic skincare accessible and transparent"

### Values
- Radical transparency in ingredients
- Environmental responsibility
- Science-backed formulations
- Cruelty-free and vegan
- Nordic simplicity

### Brand Personality
- Trustworthy and honest
- Sophisticated yet approachable
- Educational and empowering
- Minimalist aesthetic
- Environmentally conscious

## Target Audience

### Primary Audience
- **Age**: 25-40 years old
- **Gender**: Primarily female (80%), but inclusive
- **Income**: €45,000-85,000 annual
- **Location**: Urban areas in Denmark, expanding to Nordic countries
- **Education**: College-educated, professionally employed

### Psychographics
- Health and wellness conscious
- Values sustainability and ethics
- Willing to invest in quality products
- Researches ingredients and brands
- Active on social media (Instagram, TikTok)
- Follows beauty influencers and science-based skincare content

### Pain Points
- Overwhelmed by "greenwashing" in beauty industry
- Confused about which ingredients are truly beneficial
- Want effective products that align with values
- Seeking transparent brands they can trust
