## Challenges & Solutions

### Challenge 1: Product Stockout
**Issue**: Glow Essence Serum sold out for 4 days in May

**Impact**: Lost sales, some customer frustration

**Solution**: Improved inventory forecasting, implemented back-in-stock notifications

**Learning**: Better demand prediction needed for hero products

---

### Challenge 2: Paid Social CPM Increase
**Issue**: CPMs increased 22% in May due to seasonal competition

**Impact**: Required budget reallocation

**Solution**: Shifted budget to TikTok (lower CPMs), increased organic efforts

**Learning**: Diversify channels to reduce dependency on any one platform

---

### Challenge 3: Customer Education
**Issue**: Some customers confused about vitamin C tingling

**Impact**: 12% of reviews mentioned it

**Solution**: Enhanced product pages with "what to expect," better email onboarding

**Learning**: Proactive education prevents confusion and returns
