# Strategies

This directory contains strategic marketing plans, frameworks, and strategic recommendations.

## Purpose

Develop and store comprehensive marketing strategies for clients and campaigns.

## Strategy Types

- **Marketing Strategy**: Overall marketing approach and plans
- **Content Strategy**: Content themes, calendar, distribution
- **Social Media Strategy**: Platform strategies, engagement plans
- **SEO Strategy**: Search optimization approach
- **Brand Strategy**: Brand positioning and messaging
- **Growth Strategy**: Customer acquisition and retention

## Strategy Components

Each strategy should include:
1. **Situation Analysis**: Current state and context
2. **Objectives**: Clear, measurable goals
3. **Target Audience**: Who we're reaching
4. **Approach**: How we'll achieve objectives
5. **Tactics**: Specific actions and channels
6. **Metrics**: How we'll measure success
7. **Timeline**: Phasing and milestones

## Organization

```
strategies/
├── [client-name]/
│   ├── overall-marketing-strategy.md
│   ├── content-strategy.md
│   ├── channel-strategies/
│   └── quarterly-plans/
```

## Best Practices

- Root strategies in research
- Make objectives SMART
- Connect tactics to goals
- Define clear success metrics
- Include contingency plans
- Review and update regularly
