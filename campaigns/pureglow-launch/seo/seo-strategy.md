## 10. SEO Content Guidelines

### 10.1 Writing for SEO & Users

**Do**:
✅ Write for humans first, search engines second
✅ Use keywords naturally (never forced)
✅ Create comprehensive, valuable content
✅ Use clear, descriptive headings
✅ Link to authoritative sources
✅ Update content regularly

**Don't**:
❌ Keyword stuff
❌ Use thin, low-value content
❌ Duplicate content
❌ Hide text or links
❌ Use misleading titles
❌ Neglect mobile users

### 10.2 E-A-T Principles

**Expertise**:
- Cite scientific studies
- Reference dermatologists/formulators
- Show deep product knowledge
- Explain complex topics clearly

**Authoritativeness**:
- Build quality backlinks
- Get mentioned in reputable publications
- Showcase certifications
- Publish original research/data

**Trustworthiness**:
- Full transparency (on-brand!)

- Secure site (HTTPS)
