## Platform Expertise

### Instagram
- **Formats**: Feed posts, Stories, Reels, IGTV, Live
- **Best For**: Visual storytelling, lifestyle, behind-the-scenes
- **Posting Frequency**: 3-7 times per week
- **Peak Times**: Weekdays 11am-1pm, 7pm-9pm
- **Key Metrics**: Engagement rate, reach, saves

### Facebook
- **Formats**: Posts, Stories, Videos, Live, Groups
- **Best For**: Community building, long-form content, events
- **Posting Frequency**: 1-2 times per day
- **Peak Times**: Weekdays 1pm-3pm, Wed-Fri
- **Key Metrics**: Engagement, shares, video views

### Twitter/X
- **Formats**: Tweets, Threads, Spaces
- **Best For**: Real-time updates, conversations, thought leadership
- **Posting Frequency**: 5-10 times per day
- **Peak Times**: Weekdays 8am-10am, 6pm-9pm
- **Key Metrics**: Engagement rate, impressions, retweets

### LinkedIn
- **Formats**: Posts, Articles, Videos, Polls, Documents
- **Best For**: B2B content, thought leadership, professional insights
- **Posting Frequency**: 3-5 times per week
- **Peak Times**: Weekdays 7am-9am, 5pm-6pm
- **Key Metrics**: Engagement rate, post views, follower growth

### TikTok
- **Formats**: Short videos, Live
- **Best For**: Entertainment, education, trends, Gen Z/Millennial audiences
- **Posting Frequency**: 1-3 times per day
- **Peak Times**: 6am-10am, 7pm-11pm
- **Key Metrics**: Views, engagement rate, shares

### YouTube
- **Formats**: Videos, Shorts, Live, Community posts
- **Best For**: Long-form content, tutorials, storytelling
- **Posting Frequency**: 1-4 times per week
- **Peak Times**: Weekdays 2pm-4pm, weekends
- **Key Metrics**: Watch time, views, subscribers

### Content Mix Framework

**Rule of Thirds**
- 1/3 Promotional (products, services, sales)
- 1/3 Educational (tips, insights, how-tos)
- 1/3 Engaging (questions, polls, user content)

**Alternative: 80/20 Rule**
- 80% Value-driven (educational, entertaining, inspiring)
- 20% Promotional (sales, products)

### Engagement Strategy

**Types of Engagement**
- **Reactive**: Responding to comments/messages
- **Proactive**: Starting conversations, engaging with others
- **Community**: Building relationships, fostering discussion
- **Influencer**: Partnering with influencers/advocates

**Response Guidelines**
- Respond within 1-2 hours during business hours
- Use brand voice consistently
- Be helpful and friendly
- Escalate issues appropriately
- Turn negatives into positives

## Crisis Management

### Crisis Indicators
- Sudden spike in negative mentions
- Viral negative post
- Customer complaints escalating
- Product/service issues
- Controversial content

### Response Protocol
1. **Assess**: Determine severity and scope
2. **Pause**: Stop scheduled content if needed
3. **Respond**: Acknowledge quickly and authentically
4. **Escalate**: Involve appropriate stakeholders
5. **Monitor**: Track conversation and sentiment
6. **Follow-up**: Update on resolution
7. **Learn**: Document and improve

### Response Templates
- Acknowledgment: "We hear you..."
- Apology: "We sincerely apologize..."
- Action: "Here's what we're doing..."
- Escalation: "Let's connect privately..."

## Best Practices by Content Type

### Images
- High quality, well-composed
- On-brand visuals
- Minimal text overlay
- Mobile-optimized
- Proper dimensions per platform

### Videos
- Hook in first 3 seconds
- Captions for sound-off viewing
- Keep short and engaging
- Vertical format for Stories/Reels
- Clear message

### Stories
- Interactive elements (polls, questions)
- Behind-the-scenes content
- Time-sensitive offers
- Swipe-up links (when available)
- Consistent branding

### Live Content
- Promote in advance
- Have a clear topic/purpose
- Engage with viewers in real-time
- Save and repurpose
- Follow up after
