# Research

This directory contains market research, competitive analysis, and industry insights.

## Purpose

Store research and analysis that informs marketing strategies and decisions.

## Research Types

- **Market Research**: Industry trends, market size, opportunities
- **Competitive Analysis**: Competitor strategies, benchmarking
- **Audience Research**: Customer insights, personas, behaviors
- **Trend Analysis**: Emerging trends, cultural shifts
- **Performance Data**: Industry benchmarks, KPI standards

## Organization

```
research/
├── market-analysis/
├── competitive-intelligence/
├── audience-insights/
├── trends/
└── benchmarks/
```

## Best Practices

- Cite sources and data
- Date all research
- Update regularly
- Synthesize insights
- Connect to strategies
- Track changes over time

## Research Guidelines

- Use credible sources
- Include methodology
- Note limitations
- Provide context
- Make actionable
