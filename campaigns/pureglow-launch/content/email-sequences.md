### Newsletter Example 1: Ingredient Deep Dive

**Subject**: The science of hyaluronic acid (it's not what you think)

**Preview**: Let's debunk some myths about this popular ingredient

**Feature Article**:

**Hyaluronic Acid: Separating Science from Marketing**

Everyone talks about hyaluronic acid. But do you know what it actually does?

**What it is:**
A molecule (not an acid!) that holds 1000x its weight in water. Your skin naturally makes it.

**What it does:**
Pulls moisture into your skin and holds it there. Think of it as a sponge for hydration.

**Common myths:**

❌ "It penetrates deep into your skin"
Actually, most HA molecules are too large. They work on the surface—which is fine! That's where you need hydration.

❌ "All HA is the same"
Wrong. Molecular weight matters. We use multiple weights (high, medium, low) to hydrate different layers.

❌ "More is better"
Not really. Beyond 2%, you're not getting additional benefits.

**In PureGlow products:**
Our Hydra Repair Moisturizer contains 2% multi-weight HA:
- 50% low-weight (smaller molecules)
- 30% medium-weight
- 20% high-weight (larger molecules)
